What Is an ATS-Friendly Resume?

An ATS-friendly resume uses readable formatting, standard sections, role-relevant language, and clear work history so it can be reviewed more easily by screening systems and people.

TopPick Resume is designed as a resume assistant for job seekers looking for clearer structure, stronger wording, and ATS-aware presentation. It does not promise job outcomes.
