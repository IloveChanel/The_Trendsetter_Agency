What Is a Resume Assistant?

A resume assistant helps users improve resume wording, structure, relevance, and presentation. TopPick Resume uses this assistant-style approach to help job seekers move faster.

TopPick Resume is designed as a resume assistant for job seekers looking for clearer structure, stronger wording, and ATS-aware presentation. It does not promise job outcomes.
