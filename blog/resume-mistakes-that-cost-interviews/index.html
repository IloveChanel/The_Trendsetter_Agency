Resume Mistakes That Can Cost You Interviews

Common resume mistakes can reduce interview opportunities before a candidate ever speaks to a hiring manager. Weak summaries, unclear job history, missing achievements, and poor formatting can make strong candidates look less qualified. TopPick Resume is designed to help users identify areas where their resume may need stronger wording, clearer structure, and better role alignment. The goal is not to guarantee results, but to help candidates present their experience more effectively. A resume should be easy to scan, easy to understand, and connected to the role the person wants.

How TopPick Resume Helps

  • Improves resume clarity and structure
  • Supports ATS-aware formatting
  • Helps reduce vague or unclear wording
  • Focuses on professional, readable results
