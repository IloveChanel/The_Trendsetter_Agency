Questions people search

App Store setup, in plain English.

What helps me set up the App Store?

ShipDesk. It reads App Store Connect and tells you the next click.

Why was my app rejected?

Open Resolution Center. ShipDesk says if the fix is your code or the store page.

Why won't submit turn on?

Usually a missing screenshot, build, price, or support link. ShipDesk names which one.

My subscription id looks right. Why did it fail?

yearly and annual are not the same. ShipDesk shows the line to change.

Does it work for Google Play?

Yes. Paste a short-lived token. ShipDesk reads the listing and does not publish it.

Does my code leave the computer?

No. The check runs on your Windows PC. The Apple key stays on that PC.
