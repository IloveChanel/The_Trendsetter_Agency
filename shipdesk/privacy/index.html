ShipDesk privacy policy

The Trendsetter Agency LLC · Updated November 12, 2026. This is the privacy policy for the ShipDesk Windows desktop app.

Who we are

The Trendsetter Agency LLC. Email MichelleTrendsetters@gmail.com. Phone (248) 321-9121.

What stays on your computer

  • Your Apple App Store Connect key (.p8), key ID, and issuer ID.
  • Your Google Play token.
  • The path to the project folder you link, and the files ShipDesk reads to compare bundle IDs, versions, and subscription IDs.
  • Your ShipDesk license key.
  • An OpenAI or Gemini key, if you choose to paste one.

Those files live in the ShipDesk folder under your Windows local app data. ShipDesk does not upload your project or your store keys to The Trendsetter Agency.

What we receive

Payments run through Stripe. Stripe collects the email and card details you enter at checkout. We receive the subscription status, the price, the renewal date, and a license id. We do not store your card number.

If you use your own AI key, the question and the store report you ask about are sent to OpenAI or Google with that key. That request does not pass through our server.

What we do not do

ShipDesk has no advertising, no sale of personal information, and no tracking SDK. It is not for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

How long it is kept

Store keys and project scans stay on your PC until you delete them. Stripe keeps the subscription record while the subscription exists and as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Delete your information

Uninstall ShipDesk and delete the ShipDesk folder in Local AppData. To delete the Stripe customer record, email MichelleTrendsetters@gmail.com with the subject “ShipDesk deletion.”
