OFFICIAL PRIVACY DISCLOSURE: THE TRENDSETTER AGENCY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The Trendsetter Agency ("the Company," "we," "us," or "our") handles information through our mobile application and website.

1. Zero-Retention & Pass-Through Processing

We do not store, keep, sell, or monetize your data.

The Trendsetter Agency provides a "pass-through" service. When you input data for AI processing:

  • Transmission: Data is encrypted and sent directly to our 3rd-party processor (OpenAI).
  • Instant Deletion: Once the AI returns the response to your device, the data is instantly purged from our temporary transmission cache.
  • No Databases: We do not maintain a database of your personal inputs or AI prompts.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

  • AI Engine: OpenAI processes your data. We utilize enterprise-grade API settings to ensure your data is not used to train 3rd-party models. OpenAI Privacy Policy.
  • Payments: All billing and financial transactions are handled exclusively by Google Play Billing. The Trendsetter Agency never receives or stores your credit card or bank information.

3. Data Collection (Technical Disclosure)

To comply with Google Play requirements, we disclose that the following data types are transmitted ephemerally (processed only while the app is open and deleted immediately after):

  • User Content: Text/Images sent for AI analysis.
  • Device Identifiers: Used strictly for session management and fraud prevention.

4. Liability & AI Disclaimer

The Trendsetter Agency is a facilitator of 3rd-party AI services. We are not liable for the accuracy of AI-generated content (hallucinations) or the data handling practices of 3rd-party processors beyond our control.
