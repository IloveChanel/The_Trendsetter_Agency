SewSnap privacy policy

The Trendsetter Agency LLC · Updated November 12, 2026. This is the privacy policy for the SewSnap desktop app.

Who we are

The Trendsetter Agency LLC. Email MichelleTrendsetters@gmail.com. Phone (248) 321-9121.

What stays on your computer

Garment photos, body measurements, brand fit profiles, pattern pieces, and sewing guides are processed on your computer. SewSnap does not upload those files to The Trendsetter Agency.

What we receive

The subscription is billed by Stripe. Stripe collects the email and payment details you enter. We receive the subscription status, the plan, the renewal date, and a license id. We do not store your card number.

What we do not do

No advertising. No sale of personal information. SewSnap is not for children under 16.

Delete your information

Uninstall SewSnap and delete its folder on your computer. To delete the Stripe customer record, email MichelleTrendsetters@gmail.com with the subject “SewSnap deletion.”
