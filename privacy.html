Trendsetter Group LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates mobile applications (the "Apps"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Apps.

By using our Apps, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Content Data: Information you input into the App (e.g., resume content, text, job information)
  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and location data if provided
  • Job Information: Job titles, descriptions, and industry information you provide

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Unique device identifier (Device ID) used to manage app features and prevent unauthorized use
  • Purchase Information: Transaction details and purchase history managed through Google Play Billing
  • Usage Data: App interaction data, feature usage, and timestamps

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • AI Processing: To send your content to third-party AI services (e.g., OpenAI) for processing and enhancement
  • Service Delivery: To provide app functionality and generate outputs
  • Credit Management: To track purchases, credit balances, and expiration dates (where applicable)
  • Fraud Prevention: To prevent unauthorized use and sharing using device identification
  • Customer Support: To respond to inquiries and provide technical assistance
  • App Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve app functionality

3. Data Storage and Retention

3.1 User Content

We do NOT permanently store your content on our servers.

  • Content is processed in real-time through third-party AI services
  • Enhanced content is temporarily stored in your device's local storage only
  • Data is automatically deleted when you clear app data or uninstall the app
  • We do not maintain a database of user content

3.2 Purchase and Usage Data

  • Purchase data and credit balances are stored locally on your device
  • Transaction records are maintained by Google Play Billing
  • Usage logs may be stored for up to 90 days for fraud prevention

  • Purpose: AI-powered content processing and enhancement
  • Data Shared: User-provided content for processing
  • Privacy Policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy

4.2 Google Play Services

  • Purpose: In-app purchases and billing

  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4.3 Vercel (Hosting)

5. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your information:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may disclose your information only in the following circumstances:
