This Privacy Policy explains how TopPick Resume handles information when you use the app and related services.

Privacy Policy

1. Information the App Handles

  • Resume text, resume-related content, and job application materials that you choose to provide
  • Job descriptions, target roles, and comparison text that you choose to provide
  • Basic technical and diagnostic information reasonably necessary to operate the app, troubleshoot issues, maintain security, and prevent abuse
  • Purchase, subscription, or entitlement information handled by the applicable app marketplace or payment provider where relevant

2. How Information Is Used

  • To analyze and improve resume content and related materials you submit
  • To perform the requested product feature and return results back to your device
  • To support app security, reliability, fraud prevention, purchase validation, and customer support
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms, where necessary

3. Third-Party Processing and Sharing

To provide resume-editing and enhancement functionality, submitted content may be transmitted briefly to OpenAI for processing. OpenAI acts as a third-party service provider / processor used to help generate the requested output.

We do not sell your personal information or submitted resume information.

Information may also be shared where necessary with app marketplace, payment, hosting, infrastructure, analytics, security, legal, or compliance providers strictly as needed to operate, secure, and support the service.

4. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

We do not intentionally keep or permanently store submitted resume or job-description content on our own servers as part of normal product operation.

  • Submitted content may leave the device briefly only so the requested processing can be performed
  • Once processing is complete, the result is returned to your app
  • Any user-facing saved content is intended to remain on your phone unless you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app
  • Support emails or legal/compliance records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, comply with law, resolve disputes, or enforce rights

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information handled through the app. These may include encrypted transmission, limiting access, and minimizing retention.

However, no method of transmission, processing, or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

6. User Choices and Privacy Requests

  • You may stop using the app at any time
  • You may remove locally stored data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app
  • You may contact us with privacy questions, concerns, or requests at MichelleTrendsetters@gmail.com

Where applicable, we will review and respond to privacy-related requests in accordance with applicable law.

7. Children’s Privacy

TopPick Resume is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will review the matter.

8. International Processing

Depending on where you are located and where service providers operate, information may be processed in countries other than your own.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, we will update the “Last Updated” date on this page. Continued use of the app after changes become effective may constitute acceptance of the updated policy, where permitted by law.
