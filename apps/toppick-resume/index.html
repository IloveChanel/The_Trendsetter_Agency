Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 7, 2026 Last Updated: April 7, 2026

This page is the official privacy policy for TopPick Resume.

1. Information the app handles

  • Resume text and related job application content that you choose to provide
  • Job descriptions, target role details, and comparison text you choose to provide
  • Basic technical or app-functionality data reasonably needed to operate the service
  • Purchase or entitlement information handled by the relevant app marketplace where applicable

2. How the information is used

  • To analyze and improve resume content
  • To send submitted text briefly to a third-party processor so the requested feature can be performed
  • To return edited or enhanced results back to your device
  • To support security, abuse prevention, purchase validation, and app reliability where needed

3. Storage and retention

We do not intentionally keep or permanently store your submitted resume or job-description content on our own servers. Submitted content may leave the device briefly for processing, then the result is returned to the app. User-facing saved content is intended to remain on your phone unless you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Data may be shared only with service providers needed to operate the requested feature, payment or marketplace providers where applicable, or where required by law or safety/security needs.

5. Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information handled through the app, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your choices

  • You may stop using the app at any time
  • You may remove locally stored data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app
  • You may contact us regarding privacy questions
