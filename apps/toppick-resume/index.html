TopPick Resume Privacy Policy

This page is the official privacy policy for TopPick Resume, a resume-improvement application published by The Trendsetter Agency.

Effective Date: April 7, 2026 Last Updated: April 7, 2026

TopPick Resume is operated by The Trendsetter Agency. This Privacy Policy explains how TopPick Resume handles information when you use the app.

1. Information the App Handles

  • Resume text, resume content, and related job application material that you choose to provide
  • Job descriptions, target role information, and other text that you choose to provide
  • Basic technical and app-functionality data reasonably necessary to operate the app, troubleshoot issues, prevent abuse, or validate purchases
  • Purchase or entitlement information handled by the relevant app marketplace or billing provider where applicable

2. How Information Is Used

  • To analyze and improve resume content and related text that you submit
  • To briefly send submitted text to a third-party processor so the requested feature can be performed
  • To return the edited or enhanced result back to your device in the app
  • To operate app features, technical support, security, fraud prevention, and purchase validation where applicable

3. Third-Party Processing

To provide editing and enhancement features, content you submit may be transmitted briefly to a third-party service provider for processing.

We do not sell your personal information or resume information. We do not intentionally maintain a permanent server-side database of submitted resume content for ordinary product use.

4. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

We do not intentionally keep or permanently store your submitted resume or job-description content on our own servers.

  • Your submitted content may leave the device briefly only so it can be processed and returned to you
  • We do not sell that content
  • We do not intentionally retain that content as a permanent server-side user-content archive
  • Any user-facing results saved by the app are intended to remain on your phone unless you delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information.

We may share data only when necessary:

  • With service providers or processors needed to operate the requested app feature
  • With app marketplace or payment providers when needed for purchases or entitlement validation
  • If required by law or valid legal process
  • To protect rights, safety, security, and prevent fraud or abuse

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information handled through the app. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Your Choices

  • You may stop using the app at any time
  • You may remove locally stored data by deleting content in the app, clearing the app’s storage, or uninstalling the app, depending on your device
  • You may contact us with privacy questions at MichelleTrendsetters@gmail.com

8. Children’s Privacy

TopPick Resume is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, we will update the “Last Updated” date on this page.
