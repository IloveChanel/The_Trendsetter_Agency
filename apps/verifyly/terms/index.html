What Verifyly does and does not do

Verifyly gathers evidence about what you submit and describes it: what was checked, what was found, what could not be confirmed, and why the result has its state. It does not give a trust score.

"Verified" means only the specific fact stated on the result was confirmed by the named source (for example, that a link ends on an organization's published website, or that a receiving mail server authenticated the sender's domain). It does not mean the whole message, person, product, or offer is genuine.

Results depend on public sources that can be incomplete, delayed, or wrong, and on services that can be unavailable. A result that finds no warning signs is not a guarantee of safety. Verifyly is not legal, financial, or security advice. You decide what to do, and you are responsible for that decision.
