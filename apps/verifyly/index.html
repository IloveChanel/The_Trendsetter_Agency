Verifyly

Check it before you trust it.

Scam texts, fake bank emails, lookalike websites, swapped QR codes, and fake delivery notices all rely on you acting before you check. Verifyly checks first. Scan or paste what you were sent and it shows what is real, what is only claimed, what could be verified, and what to do next.

What it checks

  • Links and QR codes: follows redirects safely from our server, reads the page without running it, checks the security certificate, domain age, and public phishing and malware lists, and spots lookalike addresses.
  • Emails and messages: pressure tactics, requests for codes or gift cards, personal mailboxes pretending to be companies, and, with full email headers, whether the sender's domain was authenticated.
  • Barcodes and shipping labels: barcode check digits, public product records, and carrier tracking-number formats with a link to the carrier's own site. It never invents a sender, destination, or delivery status.
  • Documents: PDFs are read on the server and checked for hidden scripts and embedded links.

Honest results

Every check ends in one plain state (Verified, Partially verified, Needs verification, Suspicious, High-risk indicators, Contradicted, or Unknown) with the reason and the evidence behind it. There is no made-up trust score, and an AI is never allowed to decide the result.
