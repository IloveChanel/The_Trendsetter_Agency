Open ChefMaster, go to Settings, and tap Delete account. That removes the server copy of the account, recipes, pantry text, and encrypted AI login. It cannot be undone.

Without the app, email MichelleTrendsetters@gmail.com from the account address and ask for deletion. Say it is for ChefMaster.
