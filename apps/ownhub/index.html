OwnHub

In the pipeline · final stages of development · releasing soon

OwnHub is a barcode helper, not a point-of-sale screen. Scan a product barcode, take a photo of the item, set how many you have, and keep that list. Five of these. Ten of those. The count and the date stay with the list.

Scan

The phone camera reads UPC, EAN, and Code 128. A known code opens that item. A new code starts a stock card.

Count

Set the number on hand. Add five or ten. The saved date is stored with the item.

Your storage

The list lives on the phone first. You can save a copy to your own Google Drive or iCloud. The Trendsetter Agency does not host that inventory.

Export

Share a CSV for Excel, Google Sheets, or the system you already use.
