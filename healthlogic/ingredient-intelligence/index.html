One scan, a fuller picture

HealthLogic is designed to help people understand what is inside the products they use every day. Instead of reducing a product to one score, HealthLogic presents ingredient-level information in clear language so you can explore the product more deeply.

Food, beauty and pet products in one place

Use HealthLogic to explore packaged food, beauty and personal-care products, and pet products. Depending on the available product information, HealthLogic can organize declared ingredients, nutrients, vitamins, minerals, product details, and educational context about possible considerations and benefits.

Personal context matters

People do not all shop for the same reasons. HealthLogic lets you build a profile around allergies, health considerations, lifestyle preferences, and wellness goals so product information can be easier to review in the context you choose.

Not just a number

A single score cannot explain every ingredient, every product category, or every person's needs. HealthLogic is built to give you more context: what is listed, why it may be used, and what questions may be worth asking before you buy.

Clear limits are part of trustworthy information

HealthLogic provides educational product information. It does not diagnose a condition, replace a clinician, laboratory-test a product, or guarantee that every product record is complete. Always confirm the current package label and speak with a qualified professional about personal medical decisions.
