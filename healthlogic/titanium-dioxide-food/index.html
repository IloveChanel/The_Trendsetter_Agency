Food Additives and Label Transparency

Titanium dioxide in food: labels, regulation and better questions.

Learn how color additives are regulated, what a label can show and why country-by-country rules may differ.

What titanium dioxide has been used for

Titanium dioxide has been used in some foods to create or brighten a white appearance. It is a color additive, so its use is governed by food-safety rules. When it is included in a product, it may appear by name in the ingredient list.

Rules can differ by country

Different countries can set different conditions of use or update guidance at different times. A policy difference does not automatically prove one country is ignoring safety or that another has found a new danger. Read the specific rule, product category and date of the information.

Use labels for informed choices

Some people prefer products with fewer added colors. Others focus on allergies, nutrition, cost or medical dietary guidance. HealthLogic can clarify a listed ingredient, but it cannot confirm the amount in a product or perform laboratory analysis.
