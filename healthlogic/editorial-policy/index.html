Original HealthLogic analysis

HealthLogic articles are written to explain product labels, ingredient questions and public-health information in plain language. We do not copy full articles, transcripts or creator content from other websites.

Primary sources first

Where possible, HealthLogic links directly to official sources such as the FDA, EPA, NIH, HHS and other responsible agencies. We may also link to a creator or organization when their original work is relevant to a specific topic.

Videos and featured voices

Official videos and authorized creator material may be embedded or linked for informational purposes. A featured person, agency or organization does not endorse HealthLogic unless an explicit written relationship says otherwise.

Corrections matter

Product formulas, recalls, agency guidance and scientific understanding can change. Readers can report a possible error through the support page. We review reported issues and update content when reliable evidence supports a correction.

No fear-based claims

HealthLogic does not describe every unfamiliar ingredient as a toxin or claim that one ingredient explains a complex disease. We aim to distinguish listed ingredients, possible hazards, real-world exposure and evidence limits.
