The Basics

What Is a Custom AI Agent?

A custom AI agent is not a chatbot, and it isn't a one-size-fits-all piece of software. Think of it as a digital assistant or automated workflow built around one specific part of your business — the tasks that eat up your time but don't need your full attention every single time.

I can build you a custom AI agent for your specific business needs. The right solution depends on what is taking up your time. Tell me what you are doing manually, and I will help you determine what can be automated.

Depending on your workflow, a custom AI agent may be able to:

  • Read and organize information
  • Extract information from documents
  • Create or update spreadsheets
  • Sort and summarize emails
  • Research websites and leads
  • Prepare reports
  • Send routine follow-ups
  • Organize applications and forms
  • Track inventory or business information
  • Help manage appointment requests
  • Gather information from multiple sources
  • Turn scattered information into organized results

The exact capabilities depend on your workflow, the systems you already use, the permissions you're able to grant, and your specific business requirements. Every agent is scoped around what you actually need — not a fixed feature list.
